## 1. Entelect
 
**Core business:** Custom software development, technology consulting, and engineering services for corporate clients in SA and internationally.
 
**Target market:** Large corporates and enterprises (finance, retail, telecoms, public sector) in South Africa and beyond.
 
**Notable tech stack:** Enterprise Java/.NET, cloud (Azure/AWS), microservices, DevOps, data engineering. Grads work on real client projects across multiple languages.
 
**What "work ready" means to them:** Grads who can operate in a real SDLC — understand requirements, write clean code, collaborate, communicate, and accept feedback.
 
**Task format:** Online application → screening → interviews (often group/open-day activities) → 8–10 week Boot Camp with team projects and code reviews.
 
**Tools/platforms used:** Not clearly named; candidates mention a "technical test" and group coding tasks.
 
**Generic vs company-specific:** Early tests are fairly general; the Boot Camp is real-world and enterprise-tech focused.
 
**How they verify genuine work:** In-person/virtual interviews, group tasks, and Boot Camp projects code-reviewed by Tech Leads.
 
**Candidate frustrations:** Limited public feedback; process can feel light on deep algorithmic screening.
 
**Would they want our tool?** Moderately likely — they already invest in structured graduate assessment; a tool mirroring Boot Camp style could fit.
 
**Sources:** [Entelect Our Story](https://entelect.co.za/our-story/) (2026) · [ITWeb: SAGEA Awards](https://www.itweb.co.za/article/entelect-takes-home-three-awards-at-2025-sagea-awards/j5alr7QAL1r7pYQk) (2025)

## 2. Dariel Solutions
 
**Core business:** Bespoke software development and technology consulting across multiple industries.

**Notable tech stack:** Modern enterprise stacks (likely .NET/Java, cloud, data engineering). Grads work on client projects and internal platforms.
 
**What "work ready" means to them:** Determined, CS/IT-aligned grads who can grow quickly into professional engineers; fundamentals and attitude over specialisation.
 
**Task format:** Application via Ditto → additional info → panel review → interviews → offers. Non-grad devs may get a take-home coding game plus a long technical interview.
 
**Tools/platforms used:** Ditto for applications; coding game platform not publicly named.
 
**Generic vs company-specific:** Unclear; bespoke focus suggests tasks could be tailored, but unconfirmed.
 
**How they verify genuine work:** Follow-up long technical interview with architects/senior devs; for grads, interview and panel scrutiny.
 
**Candidate frustrations:** Very limited public reviews; process feels opaque outside the high-level steps.
 
**Would they want our tool?** Possibly — they already value realistic take-home tasks plus deep technical interviews.

## 3. Luno
 
**Core business:** Cryptocurrency exchange and custodial wallet platform for buying, selling, storing, and earning yield on digital assets.
 
**Target market:** Retail crypto users and active/institutional traders in emerging markets (SA, Nigeria, Malaysia, Indonesia), with local currency on-ramps.
 
**Notable tech stack:** Mobile/web trading apps, Luno Pro, blockchain integrations, security/custody systems. Grads work on backend, data, and security.
 
**What "work ready" means to them:** How you work, think, and approach problems; alignment with "Moontality" values (ownership, humility, moving fast, learning).
 
**Task format:** Application → screening call → take-home assignment + presentation → face-to-face/panel interviews (multiple rounds). Some roles include a Bryq psychometric test.
 
**Tools/platforms used:** Take-home platform not named; possibly email/GitHub.
 
**Generic vs company-specific:** Likely at least partly tailored to role (backend/crypto/data).
 
**How they verify genuine work:** Presentation of the take-home solution, plus multiple interviews probing approach and code.
 
**Candidate frustrations:** Some report generic rejections with little feedback after a significant take-home effort.
 
**Would they want our tool?** Quite likely — they already rely on take-home + presentation and value practical problem-solving.
 
**Sources:** [Luno Careers: Who We Are](https://www.luno.com/careers/who-we-are) (2026) · [Indeed: Luno Interview Process](https://za.indeed.com/cmp/Luno-2/faq/what-is-the-interview-process-like-at-luno?quid=1jcggsgmthbsj800) (2025)

## 4. Yoco
 
**Core business:** Fintech providing card readers, payment gateways, and business management tools for SMEs to accept and manage payments.
 
**Target market:** SMEs, retailers, and service providers in South Africa (expanding into Africa).
 
**Notable tech stack:** Mobile POS apps, payment APIs, backend transaction/reporting services, cloud infrastructure.
 
**What "work ready" means to them:** Technical depth (algorithms, system design, code quality) plus impact orientation, ownership, and leadership behaviours.
 
**Task format:** Recruiter screen → coding screen (LeetCode-style, timed) → system design → onsite/loop coding → behavioural/leadership round. Can run 4–8 weeks.
 
**Tools/platforms used:** Not explicitly named in SA sources.
 
**Generic vs company-specific:** Some domain flavour (merchant transactions, Yoco-specific scenarios); core is still algorithmic.
 
**How they verify genuine work:** Multi-round live interviews after the initial screen.
 
**Candidate frustrations:** Length and intensity of the loop (4–8 weeks).
 
**Would they want our tool?** Moderately likely for early screens, though they may prefer to control their own loop design.
 
**Sources:** [Yoco Careers (SA)](https://www.yoco.com/za/careers/) (2026) · [Glassdoor: Yoco SA Interviews](https://www.glassdoor.com/Interview/Yoco-South-Africa-Interview-Questions-EI_IE1982425.0,4_IL.5,17_IN211.htm) (2024–2026)

## 5. Takealot
 
**Core business:** Leading South African e-commerce marketplace and logistics platform with its own delivery network.
 
**Target market:** Online shoppers and third-party sellers in South Africa (B2C retail plus merchant marketplace).
 
**Notable tech stack:** E-commerce platform, inventory/logistics systems, data/analytics for recommendations and operations.
 
**What "work ready" means to them:** Solid technical skills, cultural fit, ability to handle e-commerce scale. 3-month hands-on grad course to production-readiness.
 
**Task format:** Application → recruiter screen → technical assessment (coding challenge, ~1–2 hours) → multiple final interviews.
 
**Tools/platforms used:** HackerRank confirmed for online coding tests (2 problems + MCQs, ~1.5 hours).

 ## Patterns Across All 15 Companies
 
- **Banks and insurers favour scenario-based tasks over pure coding tests.** Standard Bank, Nedbank, Capitec, and Old Mutual frame assessments around real banking/insurance situations (fraud complaints, client scenarios, Excel-based tasks) rather than generic algorithms...strong evidence for our FNB-aligned pitch.
- **Confirmed coding platforms:** HackerRank (Luno, Takealot, Accenture, Amazon, Microsoft), Codility (Microsoft), CoderPad-style take-home (Dariel Solutions). The rest either use unnamed/in-house tools or no coding platform at all.
- **No company fully tailors tasks to its own real projects.** Even where there's domain flavour (Yoco's merchant scenarios, Takealot's e-commerce scenarios, Standard Bank's banking case studies), none appear to build tasks directly from their actual codebase or current projects. **This is our clearest point of difference.**
- **Live follow-up is universal.** Every company verifies genuine ability through a live step (interview, presentation, code review, assessment centre) — none rely solely on an automated test. Relevant to how we think about our own integrity/verification approach.
- **Global tech giants (Amazon, Microsoft) run the most rigid processes** and are the least likely to adopt a third-party tool — useful to know which companies are realistic pilot partners vs. which are just reference points.
- **Consultancies (Deloitte, EY, Accenture) blend case studies with technical checks**, framed around service lines and client work.
- **Assessment centres with live group tasks/role-plays** (Discovery, Nedbank, Old Mutual) are common for grad programmes generally — our tool's task format could extend beyond coding-only roles if we want to broaden scope later.

# 1. Gendac

## Industry
Software development and technology.

## What the company does
Gendac builds software and digital systems for different companies. They create solutions based on what each business needs.

## Target Market
Businesses that need custom software or digital systems.

## Products / Services
- Custom software
- Mobile apps
- System integration
- Software support
- Electronic systems

## Problem(s) They Solve
Some businesses have problems that normal ready-made software cannot solve properly.

## How They Currently Solve It
They first understand the problem the company has and then build software that fits that specific need.

## Technology / Innovation
They work with software development, mobile apps, connected systems and other digital technologies.

## Relevant to Our Hackathon Problem?
Yes, very relevant.

Gendac has programmes for students and graduates where they get training and then work on real projects. This shows that companies still need to prepare graduates for the actual workplace even after they finish studying.

## Interesting Ideas / Insights
It shows that companies value practical experience, not only a qualification.

## Potential Opportunities / Gaps
Our solution could help students get this practical experience before they apply for jobs.

Students could complete tasks based on real company problems and use those tasks as proof that they can actually do the work.

## What Being Work Ready Means to Them

For Gendac, being work ready means having a good technical foundation but also being able to communicate, solve problems and understand why the work you are doing matters to the business. They value people who learn by actually building things, can work well with a team and are open to feedback and learning from more experienced people. A graduate should therefore be able to apply what they learnt at university to real problems instead of only knowing the theory.
